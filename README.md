### 👋 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science student at Quaid-i-Azam University, Islamabad**, and a freelancer who builds practical automation for businesses. I help teams **find the right leads, collect accurate data, and remove repetitive manual work** with AI agents, scrapers and custom tools.

- 🎓 BS Computer Science (3rd semester) at **QAU, Islamabad**
- 💼 Freelancing in **lead generation, data scraping and AI automation**
- 🌱 Currently learning **AI agent workflows, n8n and API integrations**
- 🤝 Open to **freelance projects and collaborations**
